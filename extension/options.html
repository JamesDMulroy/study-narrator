Study Narrator

Reads the important parts of a page, answers your questions by voice, and remembers what you save. Works with any AI you choose below.

AI

Runs codex exec in read-only sandbox mode with ephemeral sessions. Model names are the ones your Codex account offers.

Study Narrator keeps one pinned chatgpt.com tab and types into the real ChatGPT composer, so ChatGPT sees the page content and your question, and the reply is read back to you. Sign in to ChatGPT in that tab once.

With temporary chats off, ChatGPT's memory and connectors can see the page text you send.

Each new page starts a fresh chat; questions continue in it.

  






  
  

    

    

  






  
  
Examples: OpenRouter https://openrouter.ai/api/v1, Groq https://api.groq.com/openai/v1, Ollama http://localhost:11434/v1, LM Studio http://localhost:1234/v1. A custom host asks for permission once.

  
  

    

    

  






  
  

    

    

  








Voice




Edge's "Online (Natural)" voices sound best. The top entry is the automatic pick.









  

  







  
After an update, pages that were already open keep the old widget until refreshed. Allow the extension to update the widget in open tabs (an "all sites" permission used only for that) and it happens automatically.

  









Microphone



  
Web pages ask once per site. The side panel (voice quiz, chat) uses the extension's own permission; grant it here.

  







Shortcuts: Alt+R read/pause, Alt+A ask, Alt+S save highlight. Change them at edge://extensions/shortcuts. Right-click any page for "Discuss this page in ChatGPT / Claude.ai".
